GrocerieShop – Shared Shopping List App

Free real-time shared shopping list for families. Create a room, share a code, and everyone sees every change instantly.

Features

  • Shared grocery lists for the whole family
  • Real-time sync — everyone sees changes instantly
  • Join with a simple room code, no sign-up needed
  • Voice input — speak your shopping items
  • Works offline with automatic sync
  • Hebrew and English support
  • Free to use on any device
